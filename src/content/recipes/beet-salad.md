## Ingredients

- 3 large beets or 5 medium beets
- Olive oil

### Dressing

- 4 ounces of baby spinach
- Walnuts
- 1 cup of sour cream
- 2 tablespoons of lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons of honey
- 1 tablespoon of mint, minced
- A pinch of salt

## Directions

1. Set the oven to 425 degrees.
2. Wrap each beet in aluminum foil with a drizzle of olive oil and some salt.
3. Set on a baking sheet, as to catch any drippings.
4. Bake for 45 minutes for smaller ones to 1 hour and 15 minutes for larger ones.
5. When a knife stabs through them cleanly, they’re done.
6. When cold enough to peel, use paper towels to apply friction on the skin and peel it off. It’s not inedible but it’s papery and unpleasant so peel as much as possible.
7. Dice beets in bite-sized chunks.
8. Make the dressing in a bowl.
9. Assemble and mix.
