## Ingredients

- 8 ounces of spaghetti
- 1 can (200 grams) of tuna in olive oil, drained
- 2 cloves of garlic, halved
- 1 peperoncino
- 1 teaspoon of lemon juice
- 2 anchovies
- 1 tablespoon of capers
- Olive oil
- Black pepper

### Garnish

- Minced parsley

## Directions

1. Boil pasta until al dente and reserve ¼ cup of water.
2. Cook garlic and peperoncino in oil until golden.
3. Add in anchovy and capers and crumble in the sauce.
4. Toss pasta and starchy water in, lemon juice, and mix well.
5. Serve in bowl.
6. Add tuna.
7. Drizzle olive oil and grind black pepper. Mix well without breaking the tuna too much.
