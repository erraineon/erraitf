## Ingredients

- 250 grams of all-purpose flour
- 200 grams of sugar
- 1 teaspoon of baking powder
- Fennel seeds
- A small pinch of salt
- 2 extra-large eggs
- Grated zest of 1 orange or lemon
- 1 teaspoon of vanilla or almond extract
- 100 to 125 grams of almonds

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a sheet with parchment paper.
2. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, fennel seeds, and salt in a large mixing bowl.
3. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients, add the eggs, zest, and extract, and scramble them together with a fork.
4. Toast the almonds in a pan until you can smell them.
5. Gradually incorporate the flour until a pliable, firm dough forms, then mix in the almonds.
6. Divide the dough in half and shape each portion into a long, thin log (about 1 ½ inches tall). Place the logs well apart on the prepared cookie sheet.
7. Bake for about 25 minutes, until risen and lightly browned.
8. Remove from the oven and let the logs cool for 10 to 15 minutes. Lower the oven temperature to 275°F.
9. Transfer the logs to a cutting board and slice them diagonally into thick pieces.
10. Arrange the slices cut-side up on the baking sheet. For more even browning on both the top and bottom, set a wire cooling rack on the cookie sheet and arrange the slices on the rack.
11. Return to the oven for another 10 to 15 minutes, until the insides are well dried out.
12. Let the cookies cool completely before eating or storing in an airtight container.
