## Ingredients

- Grape tomatoes
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Pepper

## Directions

1. Cut tomatoes in half.
2. Lay face up on a baking sheet.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and add salt and pepper.
4. Roast for two hours and a half in the oven at 250 degrees Fahrenheit.
