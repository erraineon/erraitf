## Ingredients

- 4 ounce salmon fillet
- 1 teaspoon cajun seasoning
- 1 tablespoon butter, melted
- Lemon juice
- Vegetable oil

## Directions

1. Pat salmon dry.
2. Spread butter on one side, then brush seasoning on both sides until covered.
3. On a skillet, add vegetable oil and heat on high.
4. Add salmon to pan.
5. Reduce heat to medium to avoid overcooking. For a thin fillet, 2 minutes on each side are sufficient.
6. Add lemon juice.
