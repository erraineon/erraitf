## Ingredients

- 1 head of romaine or iceberg lettuce, shredded
- 4 ounces of blue cheese, crumbled
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 1 avocado, diced
- 3 or 4 eggs
- 6 slices of bacon
- 6 ounces of tomatoes, seeded and chopped
- 1 ½ pounds of chicken breast

### Chicken poaching brine

- 5 teaspoons of salt
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tablespoon of Italian seasoning
- 1 quart of water

### Dressing

- 8 ounces of ranch dressing or vinaigrette dressing
- OR:
  - 1 shallot
  - 1 tablespoon of dijon mustard
  - 1 ½ tablespoons of red wine vinegar
  - 3 tablespoons of olive oil
  - Salt and pepper

## Directions

1. Pressure cook chicken with the brine for 0 minutes, then natural release for 4 minutes. Ensure it’s 165F.
2. Cube chicken.
3. Add eggs to boiling water for 8 minutes. Cool, then peel and chop.
4. Cook bacon in the oven until crispy by bringing it up to 400F from a cold oven, about 18 minutes.
5. Slice shallot, season with vinegar, salt and pepper, cover for 5 minutes. Add other dressing ingredients and whisk with a fork.
6. Add shredded lettuce to a bowl, mix half the dressing.
7. Assemble the salad and drizzle the remaining dressing on top.
