## Ingredients

- 1 head of romaine or iceberg lettuce, shredded
- 4 ounces blue cheese
- 3 green onions
- 1 avocado
- 3 or 4 eggs
- 6 slices of bacon
- 6 ounces tomatoes, seeded and chopped
- 1 ½ pounds of chicken breast

### Chicken

- 5 teaspoons salt
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tablespoon Italian seasoning
- 1 quart water

### Dressing

- 8 ounces of ranch dressing or vinaigrette dressing
- OR:
  - 1 shallot
  - 1 tablespoon dijon mustard
  - 3 tablespoons red wine vinegar
  - 3 tablespoons olive oil

## Directions

1. Pressure cook chicken for 0 minutes, then natural release for 4 minutes. Ensure it’s 165F.
2. Add eggs to boiling water for 8 minutes. Cool, then peel and chop.
3. Cook bacon in the oven until crispy and crumble.
4. Slice shallot, season with vinegar, salt and pepper, cover for 5 minutes. Add other dressing ingredients and whisk with a fork.
5. Add shredded lettuce to a bowl, mix half the dressing.
6. Assemble the salad and drizzle the remaining dressing on top.
