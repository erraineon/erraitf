## Ingredients

- 8 ounces of spaghetti
- 8 ounces of small shrimp
- 3 tablespoons of olive oil
- ½ onion, minced
- 1 clove of garlic, minced
- 2 ounces of white wine
- ½ cup of heavy cream
- ¼ cup of pasta water
- 1 tablespoon of lemon juice
- Parmesan, to taste
- Chopped parsley as garnish

### Shrimp seasonings

- ½ teaspoon of salt
- ⅛ teaspoon of paprika
- ⅛ teaspoon of baking soda
- Black pepper

## Directions

1. Defrost shrimp in a ziplock bag inside a bowl for 24 hours.
2. Pat shrimp dry and season.
3. Heat up 1 tablespoon olive oil in a pan and cook shrimp at high heat for 2 minutes on each side, then set aside.
4. Add 2 tablespoons olive oil and sauté onion until golden.
5. Add garlic for a minute.
6. Deglaze with white wine and boil until it’s mostly evaporated.
7. Add heavy cream, bring to a light boil and simmer for 2 minutes.
8. Sprinkle sauce with parmesan and stir until smooth.
9. Turn off the heat and season as needed: approximately ¼ teaspoon salt and 8 grinds of black pepper.
10. Turn the heat back on to medium, stir in pasta with water, lemon, shrimp, and toss.
