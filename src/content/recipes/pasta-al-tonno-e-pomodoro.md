## Ingredients

- 12 ounces spaghetti
- 12 ounces cherry tomatoes
- 1 can of tuna
- 1 tablespoon of butter
- ½ onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- Olive oil
- Red pepper
- Black pepper
- Thyme
- Sage

## Directions

1. Melt butter and oil in a pan and fry the shallot and garlic.
2. Add tomatoes and salt, and coat in oil, until they’re soft enough to crush with a wooden spatula.
3. Add herbs and tuna and reduce the sauce.
4. Add a ladle of pasta water and the spaghetti and mix well.
