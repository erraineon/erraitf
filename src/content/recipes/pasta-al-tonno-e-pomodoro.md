## Ingredients

- 12 ounces of spaghetti
- 12 ounces of cherry tomatoes
- 1 can of tuna
- 1 tablespoon of butter
- ½ onion or a whole shallot, chopped
- 3 cloves of garlic, chopped
- Olive oil
- Red pepper
- Black pepper
- Thyme
- Sage

## Directions

1. Melt butter and oil in a pan and fry the shallot and garlic.
2. Add tomatoes and salt, and coat in oil, until they’re soft enough to crush with a wooden spatula.
3. Add herbs and reduce the sauce.
4. Add tuna towards the end to prevent it drying up.
5. Add a ladle of pasta water and the spaghetti and mix well.
