Yields 5 servings • Serve with 2.5 cups cooked rice

## Ingredients

- 1 pound of lamb leg or rump, cut ¼ inch thin

### Marinade

- 1 tablespoon of soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon of mirin or Shaoxing rice wine
- ½ teaspoon of kosher salt
- 1 ¼ teaspoons of baking soda
- 1 tablespoon of cornflour or cornstarch

### Spices

- 2 tablespoons of cumin powder
- ½ teaspoon of white sugar
- ½ teaspoon of ground Sichuan pepper

### Aromatics

- ½ cup of dried Chinese chilis
- 2 tablespoons of minced ginger
- 5 cloves of garlic, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 green pepper, sliced thin
- Scallion greens

### Garnish

- ½ cup of cilantro, chopped roughly
- 1 teaspoon of roasted sesame seeds

## Directions

1. Dry-toast 1 teaspoon of peppercorn, grind and sift to yield ½ teaspoon powder.
2. Mix lamb with marinade ingredients in a bowl and let sit for 30 minutes in the fridge, then pat dry.
3. Heat the pan until it’s around 400 degrees Fahrenheit, then add 3 tablespoons of vegetable oil and add lamb in one layer.
4. After 30 seconds, toss for one minute and remove. Repeat in batches without adding oil.
5. Lower the heat a bit, add 1 tablespoon of oil, then add chili, ginger, and garlic.
6. Shortly after, add onion and pepper and cook until soft.
7. Re-add the lamb, sprinkle spices, and toss well.
8. Remove from heat, add cilantro, sesame seeds, toss and serve.
