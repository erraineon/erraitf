## Ingredients

- 20 ounces of tortellini
- 250 grams of frozen peas, optional
- 250 grams of cubed ham
- 2 tablespoons of butter
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 cup of heavy cream
- Parmesan
- 2 twigs of thyme
- Sage

## Directions

1. Melt butter and fry the onion with 2 pinches of kosher salt.
2. Add the ham and sear for a bit.
3. Optionally add peas and cook until most liquid evaporates.
4. Add heavy cream and bring to a simmer.
5. Add herbs.
6. Melt parmesan and remove from heat.
7. Add slightly undercooked tortellini and mix well on high heat.
8. Grind some black pepper after serving.
