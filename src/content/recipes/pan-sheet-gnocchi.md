## Ingredients

- 1 pound of refrigerated gnocchi
- 1 pound of mushrooms, bite sized
- 3 sausages, casing removed, torn in bits
- 5 ounces of baby spinach
- 1 large shallot, sliced thin
- 4 scallions, cut in 1-inch lengths
- 6 tablespoons of olive oil
- ½ teaspoon of kosher salt
- Black pepper

### Seasonings

- 2 tablespoons of Dijon mustard
- 2 tablespoons of horseradish
- 1 teaspoon of honey

## Directions

1. Preheat oven at 425 degrees Fahrenheit.
2. Toss gnocchi, mushrooms, shallot and scallions with 5 tablespoons of olive oil and ½ teaspoon of kosher salt. Spread evenly.
3. Add sausage bits and black pepper on top.
4. Bake for 25 minutes, until the gnocchi is crispy on the outside.
5. Toss spinach with 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a bowl.
6. Add spinach and bake for 5 more minutes.
7. Mix the sauce in a ramekin and add half of it to the sheet, stirring to cover. If not using sausage, add 1 tablespoon of butter.
8. Serve with the rest of the sauce on top.
