## Ingredients

- 1 pound of fresh gnocchi
- 1 pound of mushrooms, bite sized
- 3 sausages, casing removed, torn in bits
- 5 ounces of baby spinach
- 1 large shallot, sliced thin
- 4 scallions, cut in 1-inch lengths
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 2 tablespoons horseradish
- 1 teaspoon honey

## Directions

1. Preheat oven at 425ºF.
2. Toss gnocchi, mushrooms, shallot and scallions with 5 tablespoons of olive oil and ½ teaspoon of kosher salt.
3. Add sausage bits and black pepper on top.
4. Bake for 25 minutes, until the gnocchi is crispy on the outside.
5. Add spinach and 1 tablespoon of olive oil, mix, and bake for 5 more minutes.
6. Mix the sauce in a ramekin and add half of it to the sheet, stirring to cover. If not using sausage, add 1 tablespoon of butter.
7. Serve with the rest of the sauce on top.
