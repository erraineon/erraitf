## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons of butter
- ½ teaspoon of baking soda
- 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
- 2 pounds of sweet onions, sliced
- ⅓ pound of carrots, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 pound of Yukon Gold or yellow potatoes, peeled and cut in 2 inch chunks
- 1 gala apple, grated
- 2 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon of grated ginger
- 1 ½ pounds of chicken thighs OR 2 pounds of chuck roast, a whole steak about 1.5 inches thick
- 3 cups of chicken stock
- Red wine to deglaze
- 95 grams of medium heat curry roux, or 120 grams for a thicker version
- Mozzarella cheese, shredded

### Flavor add-ons

- 1 tablespoon of ketchup
- 1 tablespoon of soy sauce
- ½ cup of heavy cream
- Ground pepper
- Coffee

## Directions

1. Soak potatoes for at least 15 minutes to remove starch.
2. 🍗 Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces, season with salt and pepper.

### Caramelized onion puree

1. Sauté on medium heat, then melt 3 tablespoons of butter.
2. Cook onions and baking soda until moist (5 minutes).
3. Pressure cook for 20 minutes, then quick release.
4. Sauté on medium high heat until most moisture has evaporated while stirring for 15 minutes.
5. Lower heat to medium and stir until dry brown, then remove to a bowl.

### Stew

1. 🥩 Add 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil, then lightly season beef with salt and pepper and brown for 6 minutes on each side, then set aside on a chopping board and cut a few minutes later.
2. Add 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil.
3. Cook garlic and ginger for a minute.
4. 🍗 Mix chicken in oil and sauté for about 5 minutes.
5. Deglaze with red wine or ½ cup of stock.
6. Mix carrots, apple, and potatoes, stock, and onions.
7. Pressure cook on meat/stew for 🥩 32 / 🍗 15 / 🥗 12 minutes, natural release for 🥩🍗 10 / 🥗 0 minutes.

### Curry

2. Transfer stew to the dutch oven and place on medium heat.
3. Add curry roux and flavor add-ons and mix.
4. Once reduced and thick, sprinkle mozzarella and broil in the oven for 2-3 minutes until melted and brown.
