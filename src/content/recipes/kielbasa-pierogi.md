Serving suggestion: beet salad

## Ingredients

- 1 pound of pierogi
- 14 ounces of Polska kielbasa, sliced
- 2 bell peppers
- 1 onion
- 1 tablespoon of butter
- 2 tablespoons of oil, plus extra for sautéing
- Sour cream
- Fresh dill
- Black pepper

## Directions

1. Brown the kielbasa for 3 minutes.
1. Sauté peppers and onion for 5 minutes and set aside.
1. Boil pierogi if they require it, and drain well.
1. Melt butter and oil and carefully place pierogi to maximize browning.
1. Re-add other ingredients in the pan and mix.
1. Top with sour cream, dill, and black pepper.
