Serving suggestion: beet salad

## Ingredients

- 1 pound of pierogi
- 14 ounces of Polska kielbasa
- 2 peppers
- 1 onion
- 1 tablespoon of butter
- 2 tablespoons of oil, plus extra for sautéing
- Sour cream
- Dill

## Directions

1. Sauté peppers, onion, and kielbasa and set aside.
2. Boil pierogi if they require it.
3. Melt butter and carefully place pierogi to maximize browning.
4. Re-add other ingredients in the pan and mix.
5. Top with sour cream, dill, and black pepper.
