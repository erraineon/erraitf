## Ingredients

- 1 pound fusilli or rotini
- 1 can of chickpeas
- 8 ounces mozzarella or feta
- Basil
- Mint
- Parsley
- 1 cucumber
- 1 red pepper
- Grape or cherry tomatoes
- 1 pound chicken breasts

### Dressing

- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon dijon mustard
- 3 garlic cloves minced
- 1 teaspoon herbes de Provence
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Red pepper

## Directions

1. Pressure cook chicken for 0 minutes, then natural release for 6 minutes. Ensure it’s 165F.
