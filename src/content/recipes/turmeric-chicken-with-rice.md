## Ingredients

- 5 cups cooked short-grain rice
- 2 pounds chicken thighs
- 5 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 bunch sliced scallions, white and green separated
- ½ cup cilantro leaves, chopped

### Marinade

- 4 ounces lemon juice
- 6 garlic cloves, grated
- 2 tablespoon or inches of ginger, grated
- 1 ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons turmeric
- 2 teaspoons coriander
- 1 teaspoon ground pepper

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 450F with sheet pan inside.
2. Save 1 tablespoon of the marinade to a ramekin for later.
3. Marinate the chicken at room temp for 20 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, toss the rice with ½ teaspoon of salt, scallion whites, and 3 tablespoons olive oil.
5. Add 2 tablespoons olive oil to the chicken pieces, tossing well to coat.
6. Spread the rice on the hot sheet pan.
7. Place the chicken in divots so it touches the pan, drizzle with more oil, and roast for 40 minutes.
8. Stir lemon juice and oil in the ramekin with the mixture to thin out, dab it on the rice, and mix in.
9. Top with scallion greens and cilantro.
