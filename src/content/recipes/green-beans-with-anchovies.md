## Ingredients

- 1 pound of frozen green beans
- 4 anchovy fillets
- 2 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- A few sprigs of fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 3 tablespoons of olive oil
- Black pepper

### Optionals

- Onion crisps

## Directions

1. If congealed, rinse the anchovies and pat them dry.
2. Boil beans in salted water until tender but still slightly firm.
3. Sauté chopped garlic, onion, and anchovies in oil until soft.
4. Add 2 ounces of cooking water to the pan.
5. Drain beans and add to the pan.
6. Turn the heat back on and sauté for a couple of minutes until most water has evaporated.
7. Add parsley.
