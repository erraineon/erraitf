## Ingredients

- 1 pound frozen green beans
- 4 anchovy fillets
- 2 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- A few sprigs of fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 3 tablespoons olive oil

### Optionals

- Onion crisps

## Directions

1. If congealed, rinse the anchovies and pat them dry.
2. Boil beans in salted water until tender but still slightly firm.
3. Sauté chopped garlic and onion in oil until soft.
4. Add anchovy fillets and let them melt into the oil.
5. Turn off the heat and add chopped parsley.
6. Add 2 ounces of cooking water to the pan.
7. Drain beans and add to the pan.
8. Turn the heat back on and sauté for a couple of minutes until most water has evaporated.
