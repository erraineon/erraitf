## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons of vegetable oil
- 3 fresh Thai bird peppers, sliced thin
- 2 dried árbol chilies, chopped
- 1 tablespoon of Sichuan pepper, toasted and ground
- 3 tablespoons of ginger, minced
- 3 tablespoons of garlic, minced
- 8 ounces of ground pork
- 2 tablespoons of Sichuan spicy bean sauce
- ⅔ cup of low sodium chicken broth
- 1 pound of silken tofu, in 1 inch cubes
- ¼ cup of water
- 1 ½ teaspoons of cornstarch
- ¼ teaspoon of sesame oil
- ¼ teaspoon of sugar
- Scallion greens, chopped

## Directions

1. Toast the chilies in 3 tablespoons oil on low heat for about 5 minutes until fragrant.
2. Transfer oil and peppers to a ramekin.
3. Add 3 more tablespoons of oil on low heat.
4. Fry most of the ground peppercorn for about 15 seconds.
5. Fry ginger, garlic, and Thai bird chilies for 1 minute.
6. Turn heat to high and fry pork until brown.
7. Add spicy bean sauce and stir for about a minute.
8. Add chicken broth and simmer for a minute.
9. Simmer the tofu in lightly salted water for 2 minutes and drain to firm it up.
10. Add tofu and stir gently, letting cook for 3-5 minutes.
11. Gently move tofu aside, add water/cornstarch mixture and stir. Simmer until it thickens.
12. Add chili oil and stir.
13. Add sesame oil, sugar, and scallions and stir.
14. Serve with a sprinkle of peppercorn powder.
