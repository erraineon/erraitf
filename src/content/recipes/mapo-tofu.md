Serving suggestion: 3 cups cooked rice

## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 3 fresh Thai bird peppers, sliced thin
- 2 dried arbor chilies, chopped
- 1 tablespoon Sichuan pepper
- 3 tablespoons ginger, minced
- 3 tablespoons garlic, minced
- 8 ounces ground pork
- 2 tablespoons spicy bean sauce
- ⅔ cup low sodium chicken broth
- 1 pound silken tofu, in 1 inch cubes
- ¼ cup water
- 1 ½ teaspoons cornstarch
- ¼ teaspoon sesame oil
- ¼ teaspoon sugar
- Scallion greens, chopped

## Directions

1. Toast the chilies in 3 tablespoons oil on low heat for about 5 minutes until fragrant.
2. Transfer oil and peppers to a ramekin.
3. Add 3 more tablespoons of oil on medium heat.
4. Fry ground peppercorn for about 15 seconds.
5. Fry ginger for 1 minute.
6. Fry garlic for 1 minute.
7. Turn heat to high and fry pork until brown.
8. Add spicy bean sauce and stir.
9. Add chicken broth and simmer for a minute.
10. Add water/cornstarch mixture and stir. Simmer until it thickens.
11. Add chili oil and stir.
12. Add tofu and stir gently, letting cook for 3-5 minutes.
13. Add sesame oil, sugar, and scallions and stir.
14. Serve with a sprinkle of peppercorn powder.
