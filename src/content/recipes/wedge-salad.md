Yields 2 large servings

## Ingredients

- 1 head iceberg lettuce, with outer leaves and bottom removed, quartered in wedges
- 4 slices of bacon, sliced or crumbled
- ½ red onion
- 2 small tomatoes, diced
- 2 ounces breadcrumbs
- ½ cups blue cheese dressing

### Dressing

- ⅓ cups light sour cream
- ⅓ cups buttermilk
- ⅓ cups mayonnaise
- 40 grams blue cheese or gorgonzola
- Black pepper
- 3 teaspoons red wine vinegar
- ½ teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- ¼ teaspoon garlic salt
- Mint, chives, scallions, parsley or herbs

## Directions

1. Whisk dressing together and let it sit in the fridge for at least half an hour.
2. Separate onion rings and quick-pickle in red wine vinegar, covered for at least 10 minutes.
3. Place diced tomatoes in a strainer over a bowl with some salt to extract moisture.
4. Remove outer leaves and bottom, quarter the lettuce into four wedges, rinse and let dry on paper towels.
5. Assemble the salad.
