## Ingredients

- 8 ounces of pasta
- 2 egg yolks
- 25 grams of parmesan & pecorino mix
- 4 ounces of guanciale or bacon, sliced
- Abundant black pepper

## Directions

1. Whisk egg yolks with one third of the cheese and black pepper.
2. Fry the meat at medium-low heat, as to not over-render the fat which results in bitter flavors.
3. Remove once crispy, leaving about 2 tablespoons of rendered fat.
4. Add a ladle of starchy water to the pan with the rendered fat, scraping the bottom with a wooden spoon, and reduce slightly.
5. Remove the pan from heat. Toss the pasta in the warm pan with the fat.
6. Stir in the egg mix, remaining cheese, and crispy meat, while gradually pouring ½ cups of starchy water and tossing until creamy.
