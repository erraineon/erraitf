## Ingredients

- 2 beaten eggs
- 12 oz shrimp, smaller
- 1 diced onion
- 1 cup peas and carrots blend
- Scallions
- Cooked rice (from the day before)

### Seasonings

- ½ tsp salt (or to taste)
- ½ tsp sugar
- ½ tsp sesame oil
- ½ tsp ground white pepper
- 1 Tbsp Shaoxing wine (swap to mirin)
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce

### Aromatics

- 4 cloves of garlic
- 1 thumb of ginger

## Directions

1. Prep ahead: Defrost shrimp for 24 hours. Cook rice the day before.
2. Pat the shrimp dry.
3. In pan: 1 tbsp of oil on medium-low heat and eggs until scrambled, and transfer to a bowl. Clean pan if needed.
4. In pan: 1 tbsp of oil on high heat and shrimp until seared, and transfer to a bowl.
5. In pan: 1 tbsp of oil on medium heat and onions, add some salt, then peas and carrots until sauteed, and transfer to a bowl.
6. In the braiser, preheated to 400f: Add 4 tbsp of oil, then aromatics and rice until fried.
7. Re-add vegetables to the pan, then add seasonings, shrimp, eggs and scallions, Shaoxing wine, while mixing in-between.
