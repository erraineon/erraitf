## Ingredients

- 2 beaten eggs
- 12 ounces of small shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 diced onion
- 1 cup of peas and carrots blend
- Scallion greens, sliced
- 3-4 cups cooked rice (from the day before, corresponds to 1 ½ cups dry rice)
- 7 tablespoons of vegetable oil

### Seasonings

- ½ teaspoon of salt (or to taste)
- ½ teaspoon of sugar
- ½ teaspoon of ground white pepper
- 1 tablespoon of Shaoxing wine (swap to mirin)
- 1 tablespoon of soy sauce
- ½ teaspoon of sesame oil

### Aromatics

- 4 cloves of garlic, grated
- 1 thumb of ginger, grated

## Directions

1. Prep ahead: Defrost shrimp for 24 hours. Cook rice the day before.
2. Pat the shrimp dry.
3. In a stainless steel pan: heat 1 tablespoon of oil on medium-low heat and eggs until scrambled, and transfer to a bowl. Clean pan if needed.
4. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil on high heat and shrimp for about 1-2 minutes until seared, and transfer to a bowl.
5. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil on medium heat and onions, add some salt, then peas and carrots until sauteed, and transfer to a bowl.
6. Break rice clumps with damp hands and toss with 2 tablespoons of oil in the bowl to coat.
7. In the enameled braiser, preheated to 400°F: add 2 tablespoons of oil and rice for about 3 minutes until fried.
8. Make a well in the center and add aromatics for a few seconds.
9. Re-add vegetables to the pan, then add dry seasonings, shrimp, eggs and scallions, while mixing in-between.
10. Add Shaoxing wine and soy sauce around the perimeter of the pan.
11. Add sesame oil with the heat off.
