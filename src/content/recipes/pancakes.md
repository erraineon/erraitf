Yields 6 large pancakes

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 cups buttermilk, plus 1-2 extra tablespoons if using higher protein content flour like KA.
- ¼ cup sour cream
- 2 large eggs
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
- 1-2 teaspoons vegetable oil

### Optionals

- Chocolate chips in the batter
- Nutella and banana
- Honey, sour cream, and raisins

## Directions

1. Set oven rack to middle position and heat it to 200 degrees. Spray wire rack with vegetable oil and place inside.
2. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl.
3. In another bowl, whisk buttermilk, sour cream, eggs, and butter.
4. Heat 1 teaspoon vegetable oil in skillet until shimmering, then wipe with paper towels leaving a film. Repeat for each pancake.
5. Add ¼ to ½ cup of batter, and cook until edges are set, first side is golden brown, and bubbles are breaking, 2 to 3 minutes on each side.
