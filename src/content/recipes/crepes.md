## Ingredients

- 2 large eggs
- 1 ½ cup of milk
- 1 cup of flour
- 2 tablespoons of butter, plus more for greasing the pan
- ¼ teaspoon of kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons of sugar
- 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract

## Directions

### Make ahead

1. Melt butter by zapping it in a ramekin for 7-10 seconds in the microwave, mixing, and repeating. Let cool.
2. In a blender, combine all of the ingredients except the butter for 30 seconds, increasing the speed from 1 to 5.
3. Add melted butter and pulse for 5 seconds.
4. Place the crepe batter in the refrigerator for 30 minutes to 24 hours.

### Cook

This assumes using the induction cooktop with controllable temperature.

1. Heat up pan on medium low setting.
2. Grease the pan with some butter.
3. Add 4 ounces of batter, and swirl to coat the bottom.
4. Turn heat to 320°F, flip once it starts bubbling and the edges start curling.

## Notes

- **Preservation:** Once the crepes have cooled, you can stack them, separated with sheets of parchment paper, and store in zip-top plastic bags in the refrigerator for several days or in the freezer for up to 1 month. When using frozen crepes, thaw on a rack before gently peeling apart.
- 3 ounces of batter is a bit too thin.
