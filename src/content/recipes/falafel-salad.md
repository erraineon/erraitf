## Ingredients

- 5 falafels
- 2 cups of lettuce, chopped
- ¾ cup of cucumber, sliced
- ¾ cup of tomatoes, diced
- Avocado, sliced
- Feta cheese, crumbled
- Olives, pitted
- Mint, chopped

### Dressing

- 2 tablespoons of tahini
- ½ teaspoon of salt
- 1 tablespoon of lemon juice
- 10 leaves of mint
- 1 tablespoon of olive oil
- 2 tablespoons of water (or add until runny)
- 1 teaspoon of mustard
- 2 teaspoons of honey
- 1 roasted red bell pepper

## Directions

1. If using peppers: broil for 15 minutes, turning until blackened.
2. Steam them in a plastic-covered bowl for 15 minutes.
3. Heat falafel up in the oven at 400°F.
4. Peel the black skin from the pepper and stem and seeds.
5. Process peppers in the processor with the other dressing ingredients for a minute. Scrape down the sides of the bowl, then repeat.
6. Compose salad and drizzle.
