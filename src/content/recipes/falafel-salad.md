700 calories • 2 servings

## Ingredients

- 5 falafels
- 2 cups of lettuce
- ¾ cup of cucumber
- ¾ cup of tomatoes
- Avocado
- Feta cheese
- Olives
- Mint

### Dressing

- 2 tablespoons tahini
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 2 grams of mint
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 tablespoons water (or add until runny)
- 1 teaspoon mustard
- 2 teaspoons honey
- 1 red pepper, optional

## Directions

1. If using peppers: broil for 15 minutes, turning until blackened.
2. Steam them in a plastic-covered bowl for 15 minutes.
3. Heat falafel up in the oven at 400F.
4. Peel the black skin from the pepper and stem and seeds.
5. Process peppers in the processor with the other dressing ingredients for a minute. Scrape down the sides of the bowl, then repeat.
6. Compose salad and drizzle.
