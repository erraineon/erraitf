## Ingredients

- 2 cups of oven roasted tomatoes
- 8 thin lemon slices
- 3 cloves of garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons of lemon juice
- 1 pound of pasta
- 2 tablespoons of fresh thyme
- 2 cups of arugula
- 6 ounces of mozzarella balls
- ½ cup of toasted pine nuts
- 1 cup of fresh basil
- Black pepper

### Optional

- 6 ounces of black olives
- 1 bell pepper
- 6 ounces of mushrooms

## Directions

1. Drizzle lemon slices with olive oil and bake at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for 15 minutes or until golden brown, then mince.
2. In a cold bowl, combine oil, garlic, lemon juice, and 1t salt.
3. Once the pasta is al dente, toss into the pot with ¼ cup cooking water and the arugula, until wilted.
4. Add the rest of the ingredients, toss, and serve.
