## Ingredients

- 8 ounces of pasta, spaghetti preferred
- 6 cloves of garlic, sliced thin
- 2 dried peperoncini
- 4 tablespoons of olive oil
- 2 pinches of kosher salt
- Black pepper
- 30 grams of parmesan[^1]

## Directions

1. Add garlic and peperoncini to cold oil and cook on low heat until golden.
2. Add a ladle of pasta water and strained pasta.
3. Mix well with the heat off and add parmesan and black pepper.

[^1]: Optional, but my bf likes it better with.
