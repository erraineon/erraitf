## Ingredients

- 1 pound of sirloin flap
- 6 cloves of garlic
- 1 ½ tablespoons of butter
- 3 tablespoons of vegetable oil

### Marinade

- 2 tablespoons of oil
- 3 tablespoons of soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons of Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon of Italian seasoning
- 1 tablespoon of garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon of white pepper
- 1 ½ tablespoons of brown sugar
- A dash of hot sauce

## Directions

1. Whisk marinade ingredients.
2. Cube steak in 1 inch pieces, try to keep width consistent so cooking is even.
3. Marinade for around 8 hours in a ziplock bag in the fridge, squeeze air out.
4. Pat dry with paper towels.
5. Heat induction cooktop at 275 degrees Fahrenheit. When the pan reads 300 degrees Fahrenheit, add garlic and sizzle until golden, then remove.
6. Turn the cooktop to 350 degrees Fahrenheit. When the pan is around 400 degrees Fahrenheit, place steak tips about 1 inch apart to not overcrowd. Cook in batches.
7. Sear for 2 minutes per side.
8. Remove on a cutting board. Look for 130 degrees Fahrenheit meat temp.
9. Once all the steak is cooked, lower the temperature to 275 degrees Fahrenheit, fully melt butter with fried garlic and re-add the steak tips (don’t pour the juices back into the pan). Baste and serve.
