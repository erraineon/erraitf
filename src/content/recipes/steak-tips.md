## Ingredients

- 1 pound sirloin flap
- 6 garlic cloves
- 1 ½ tablespoon butter
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil

### Marinade

- 2 tablespoons oil
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon Italian seasoning
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoons white pepper
- 1 ½ tablespoons brown sugar
- A dash of hot sauce

## Directions

1. Whisk marinade ingredients.
2. Cube steak in 1 inch pieces, try to keep width consistent so cooking is even.
3. Marinade for around 8 hours in a ziplock bag in the fridge, squeeze air out.
4. Pat dry with paper towels.
5. Heat induction cooktop at 275F. When the pan reads 300F, add garlic and sizzle until golden, then remove.
6. Turn the cooktop to 350F. When the pan is around 400F, place steak tips about 1 inch apart to not overcrowd. Cook in batches.
7. Sear for 2 minutes per side.
8. Remove on a cutting board. Look for 130F meat temp.
9. Once all the steak is cooked, lower the temperature to 275F, fully melt butter with fried garlic and re-add the steak tips (don’t pour the juices back into the pan). Baste and serve.
