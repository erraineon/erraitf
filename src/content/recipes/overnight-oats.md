350 calories

## Ingredients

- ½ cup of rolled oats (45 grams)
- ½ cup of milk (120 grams)
- ¼ cup of greek yogurt (60 grams)
- 1 tablespoon of chia seeds
- ¾ tablespoon of honey
- ¼ teaspoon of cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon of vanilla extract
- A pinch of salt

### Optionals

- ½ banana
- Nutella
- Berries
- Walnuts
- Dates

## Directions

1. Mix in an 8-ounce jar and refrigerate overnight for up to 5 days.
