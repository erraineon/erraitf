300 calories

## Ingredients

- ½ cup rolled oats (45g)
- ½ cup milk (80g)
- ¼ cup greek yogurt (60g)
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- ¾ tablespoon honey
- ¼ teaspoon cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
- A pinch of salt

### Optionals

- ½ banana
- Nutella
- Berries
- Walnuts
- Dates

## Directions

1. Mix in an 8oz jar and refrigerate overnight for up to 5 days.
