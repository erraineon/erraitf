## Ingredients

- 1 pound of 80-20 ground beef
- 4 sesame buns
- 4 cheese singles
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1 tomato
- Pickles
- Shredded lettuce
- Salt and pepper
- Mayonnaise
- Vegetable oil

### For the sauce

- 25 grams of mayonnaise
- 20 grams of mustard
- 15 grams of tomato paste
- 1 pickle, minced
- 5 grams of capers, rinsed
- ½ teaspoon of garlic powder
- A pinch of salt
- 3.5 grams of white vinegar
- ¼ teaspoon of liquid smoke
- 10 grams of sunflower oil, poured while mixing

## Directions

1. Whisk together sauce ingredients.
1. Cut tomatoes, salt them, and wrap in a tea towel to dry.
1. Keep the ground beef cold for as long as possible, and work minimally with no seasonings. Divide in 4 equal patties, flatten to be larger than the bun, and add a divot in the center.
1. Spread mayo on the buns and toast them in the skillet, then spread sauce on buns when you have time.
1. Heat up skillet to around 400F. Add a splash of oil.
1. Season patties with salt and pepper on both sides and immediately add to skillet.
1. Cook for 3 minutes.
1. Flip burgers and cook for 2 minutes or until it’s at 135F.
1. Turn heat off, add cheese and melt for 1 minute, then let rest for a couple of minutes.
1. Assemble.
