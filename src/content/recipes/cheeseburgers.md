Yields 4 sandwiches, 530 calories each

## Ingredients

- 1lb 80-20 ground beef
- 4 sesame buns
- 4 cheese singles
- 1 small onion
- 1 tomato
- Pickles
- Shredded lettuce
- Sauce of choice

### Equipment

- Meat thermometer
- Metal spatula
- Splatter guard

## Directions

1. Cut tomatoes, salt them, and wrap in a tea towel to dry.
2. Keep the ground beef cold for as long as possible, and work minimally with no seasonings. Flatten to be larger than the bun, and add a divot in the center.
3. Spread mayo on the buns and toast them in the skillet.
4. Heat up skillet to around 400F.
5. Season patties with salt and pepper on both sides.
6. Cook for 3 minutes.
7. Spread your sauce on buns while you wait.
8. Flip burgers and cook for 2 minutes or until it’s at 160F.
9. Flip again, turn heat off, add cheese and melt for 1 minute.
