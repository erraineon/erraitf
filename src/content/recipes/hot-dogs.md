## Ingredients

- 4 beef hot dogs
- 4 hot dog buns
- 4 cheese singles
- 1 extra large onion, sliced thin
- 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
- ½ cup of water
- 2 tablespoons of tomato paste

### Onion sauce seasonings

- 1 ½ teaspoons of hot sauce
- 1 teaspoon of yellow mustard
- 1 teaspoon of sugar
- 1 teaspoon of chili powder
- 1 teaspoon of salt
- ¼ teaspoon of black pepper
- ¼ teaspoon of cayenne pepper

## Directions

1. Heat oil on medium heat.
2. Add onion until soft and translucent.
3. Add onion spice mix, reduce heat and cook for 5 minutes.
4. Add water and tomato paste and simmer until reduced, around 20 minutes. Add a splash of water if it's too dry.
5. Preheat broiler for 5 minutes.
6. Slice hot dogs diagonally with shallow diagonal slits.
7. Place hot dogs on a tray covered with aluminum foil and bake for 5 minutes, until blistered.
8. Turn and bake for 2 more minutes.
9. Add cheese to hot dog buns and bake for 2 minutes or until crispy.
10. Assemble hot dogs.
