Serving suggestions: pinto beans, potato salad

## Ingredients

- 4 beef hot dogs
- 4 hot dog buns
- 4 cheese singles
- 1 extra large onion, sliced thin
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- ½ cup water
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste

### Onion spice mix

- 1 ½ teaspoons hot sauce
- 1 teaspoon yellow mustard
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper

## Directions

1. Heat oil on medium heat.
2. Add onion until soft and translucent.
3. Add onion spice mix, reduce heat and cook for 5 minutes.
4. Add water and tomato paste and simmer until reduced, around 20 minutes.
5. Preheat boiler for 5 minutes.
6. Slice hot dogs diagonally.
7. Place hot dogs on a tray covered with aluminum foil and bake for 5 minutes.
8. Turn and bake for 2 more minutes.
9. Add cheese to hot dog buns and bake for 2 minutes or until crispy.
10. Assemble hot dogs.
