## Ingredients

- 1 cup polenta
- 4 cups of water or broth, can also use some milk or heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 2 tablespoons of butter
- Grated parmesan cheese
- Ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Rub instant pot lightly with olive oil to prevent sticking.
2. Add polenta, broth, and salt to instant pot and mix well.
3. Cook at high pressure for 10 minutes.
4. Natural release for 10 minutes, then fast release.
5. Open and mix with whisk.
6. Add remaining ingredients.
7. If it thickens, add more liquid and whisk.

## Notes

- **Tuna sauce:** Sauté sliced garlic, 1 tablespoon rinsed & chopped capers, 2 anchovies, 1 tablespoon tomato paste; deglaze with 2 ounces cognac; blister 6 ounces halved cherry tomatoes; simmer in ½ cup broth; add 2 cans of tuna and sliced basil.
