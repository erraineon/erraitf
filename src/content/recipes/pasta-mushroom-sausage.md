## Ingredients

- 1 pound of pasta
- 2 tablespoons of olive oil
- 4 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 onion, minced
- 1 pound of baby bella mushrooms, sliced
- 1 pound of good quality sausages, crumbled
- ½ cup of white wine
- 1 cup of grated Parmesan
- Sage and rosemary
- Red pepper flakes
- Paprika

## Directions

1. Brown crumbled sausage in oil, then remove and set aside.
2. Sauté mushrooms, adding salt after they start to brown.
3. Add onion, garlic, herbs, and spices and cook until softened.
4. Deglaze with wine and return sausage to the pan.
5. Toss pasta, starchy water and Parmesan in, and stir with the heat off.
