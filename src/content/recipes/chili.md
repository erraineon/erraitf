## Ingredients

- ½ pound of bacon, chopped
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 3 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 pound of ground beef, 90%
- 1 ¼ cups of beef broth
- One 15-ounce can each of each dark red kidney beans and black beans, lightly rinsed and drained
- 14.5-ounce can of fire-roasted tomatoes
- 2 ounces of tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon of Worcestershire sauce
- 2 ounces of jalapeños in brine OR 8 ounces of fire-roasted chilis

### Spices

- 1 tablespoon of brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons of chili powder
- 1 ½ teaspoons of paprika
- 1 teaspoon of cumin
- 1 teaspoon of onion powder
- ¾ teaspoon of black pepper
- 1 teaspoon of salt
- ½ teaspoon of cayenne

### Toppings

- Sour cream
- Shredded cheese
- Corn chips

## Directions

1. Cook bacon until crispy, dry on paper plate, drain most grease.
2. Cook onion and pepper until soft.
3. Cook garlic for a minute.
4. Cook beef until mostly browned.
5. Add spices and tomato paste and bloom for a minute, then add liquids and stir.
6. Add everything else, including half of the bacon.
7. Boil for 2 minutes.
8. Simmer for 30 minutes uncovered, stir occasionally.
9. Add the rest of the bacon as a crispy topping.
