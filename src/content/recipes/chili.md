## Ingredients

- ½ pound bacon, chopped
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 pound ground beef, 90%
- 1 ¼ cup beef broth
- 15 ounce cans of dark red kidney and black beans, lightly rinsed and drained
- 14.5 ounce can fire roasted tomatoes
- 2 ounce tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 2 ounces jalapeños in brine OR 8 ounces fire roasted chilis

### Spices

- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 ½ teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- ¾ teaspoon black pepper
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon of cayenne

### Toppings

- Sour cream
- Shredded cheese
- Corn chips

## Directions

1. Cook bacon until crispy, dry on paper plate, drain most grease.
2. Cook onion and pepper until soft.
3. Cook garlic for a minute.
4. Cook beef until 50% browned.
5. Add spices and stir.
6. Add everything else, including the bacon.
7. Boil for 2 minutes.
8. Simmer for 30 minutes uncovered, stir occasionally.
