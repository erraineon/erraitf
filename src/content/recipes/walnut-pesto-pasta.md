Serving suggestion: green beans boiled in pasta water

## Ingredients

- 1 pound pasta
- 3 cups of basil (1 large bunch)
- 3 cloves of garlic
- 100 grams parmesan/pecorino mix
- 1 cup walnuts
- ½ cup olive oil
- Juice from half a lemon
- Chili flakes

## Directions

1. Pulse garlic until minced. Add the rest and pulse but retain some texture.
2. Mix in with cooked pasta in the bowl.
