Also known as dry sautéed broccoli

## Ingredients

- 1 pound fresh broccoli, cut into florets OR frozen broccoli, thawed and dried
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- White wine to deglaze
- Peperoncino to taste

### Optionals

- Thyme
- Fresh dill
- Lemon juice

## Directions

1. Get garlic browned in a pan with oil and remove.
2. Add frozen broccoli directly to pan and stir until they’re covered, trying not to break the florets.
3. Cover and let it simmer.

## Notes

- Frozen broccoli retain too much water, taking too long to cook and turning the “dry” sauté into steaming.
