Also known as dry sautéed broccoli.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound of fresh broccoli, cut into florets
- 4 tablespoons of olive oil
- ½ teaspoon of salt
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 ounces of white wine
- Peperoncino

## Directions

1. Add oil, peperoncino, and garlic in a pan on medium-low heat.
2. Remove garlic when it's infused.
3. Add broccoli and salt to pan and stir until they’re covered, trying not to break the florets.
4. Cover and let it simmer for about 15 minutes.
5. Deglaze with white wine and reduce.
