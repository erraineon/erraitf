Pair with polenta, spinach, or broccoli.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound steak cut of choice
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons butter, cubed as to melt faster
- 3 garlic cloves, smashed
- Thyme sprigs
- Lemon juice

## Directions

1. Between 1 to 12 hours ahead of time, pat steak dry with paper towels.
2. Massage ¼ teaspoon of salt on each side.
3. Place on a wire rack in fridge uncovered.
4. Remove 20-45 minutes ahead of cooking to return to room temperature, and pat dry.
5. Heat skillet on medium-high with no oil. With the induction cooktop, I use 390-410ºF.
6. Lightly moisten and microwave a plate for 30 seconds, then pat dry.
7. Lightly brush vegetable oil on both sides of the steak.
8. For a 1-inch cut, sear for about 2-3 minutes per side for medium-rare (130ºF).
9. 2 minutes before it’s done cooking, add baste ingredients and spoon over steak.
10. Transfer to warm plate and grind fresh black pepper.
11. Tent with foil and let rest for 5 minutes before cutting and serving.

## Notes

- Preferably ribeye, filet mignon, New York strip, or top sirloin cap. Inside skirt is too tough. Don’t trim the fat off but use it for rendering.
