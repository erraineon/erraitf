## Ingredients

- 8 ounces of pasta, penne rigate preferred
- 4 ounces of smoked salmon, roughly chopped
- ¾ of a cup of heavy cream
- 1 tablespoon of butter
- 1 shallot, minced
- 2 ounces of Vodka
- 2 teaspoons of lemon juice
- Black pepper
- Cayenne pepper
- Nutmeg
- Paprika

### Optionals

- 4 ounces cherry tomatoes
- Capers, rinsed and roughly chopped
- Thyme
- Sage

## Directions

1. Melt butter in a pan and fry the shallot and capers, if using them.
2. If using tomatoes, fry them until they fall apart.
3. Mix in herbs and salmon until it loses its raw color.
4. Add vodka and reduce.
5. Add the heavy cream and simmer until reduced.
6. Add spices. Aim for a savory sauce as the pasta will mellow it out.
7. Add pasta, lemon juice, and a ladle of pasta water and stir.
