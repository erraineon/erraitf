## Ingredients

- 320 grams risotto
- 1 liter broth
- 1 shallot, sliced
- 1 pound sausage
- 1 garlic clove, cut in half
- 8 ounces mushrooms, rinsed
- 6 tablespoons white wine
- 5-6 twigs of thyme
- 3 tablespoons butter, cold
- 40 grams parmesan
- A drizzle of truffle oil

### Cookware

- Frying pan for mushrooms
- Dutch oven for sausage and rice
- Medium pot for broth
- Hand blender for mushroom cream

## Directions

1. Heat up broth in a pot and keep warm.
2. Cook mushrooms with 3 tablespoons olive oil and garlic.
3. Deglaze with 3 tablespoons of white wine and add salt.
4. Cook for 10 minutes and remove from the pan.
5. Blend one third of the mushrooms with a tablespoon of broth for a cream.
6. Cook sausage in small chunks in 1 tablespoon of oil until browned.
7. Add shallot and cook until softened.
8. Add thyme leaves and deglaze with 3 tablespoons of white wine for about 5 minutes and remove from the pan.
9. Add rice and toast for 3 minutes, stirring often.
10. Add a ladle of hot broth and wait until it’s absorbed. Stir gently and occasionally for 18 minutes, and add sausage and mushrooms 15 minutes in.
11. Stir well and add salt if needed.
12. Remove from heat and add butter and parmesan.
13. Close lid for a couple of minutes, stir, add pepper and some more thyme, and serve.
