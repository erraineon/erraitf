## Ingredients

- 1 pound of baby spinach
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 4 tablespoons of olive oil
- Salt
- Pepper
- Lemon juice

## Directions

1. Rinse spinach in water without drying out.
2. Fry garlic until it begins to give off fragrance.
3. Add raw spinach leaves and toss well with a sprinkle of salt.
4. Simmer for a couple of minutes, until reduced and tender.
5. Adjust seasoning and serve.
