## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons of olive oil
- 2 shallots, sliced thin
- 1 pound of mushrooms, sliced (can be frozen)
- ¼ cup of heavy cream
- Salt and pepper
- 1 tablespoon of butter
- A splash of white wine

## Directions

1. Toss mushrooms in a dry pan over medium heat and reduce until not brothy.
2. Add oil, butter, salt and pepper and sauté until brown, about 3 minutes.
3. Add shallots.
4. Add white wine to deglaze and evaporate for about 1 minute.
5. Add heavy cream and simmer until thick.
