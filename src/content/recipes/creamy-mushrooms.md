## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 shallots, sliced thin
- 1 lb mushrooms
- ¼ cup of heavy cream

## Directions

1. Cook shallots.
2. Toss mushrooms and reduce until it’s not broth.
3. Add salt and pepper and the heavy cream.
