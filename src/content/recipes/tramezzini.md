## Ingredients

### Common ingredients

- Pan de mie, pancarrè, or pullman loaf, sliced the long way, with crusts removed.
- Mayonnaise on each bread slice. A dry tramezzino is no good.

### Prosciutto & formaggio

Yields 8 square sandwiches.

- ⅔ pound of deli ham
- ⅓ pound of sliced mozzarella or gruyere
- 1 pound of sliced mushrooms
- Cream cheese
- Herbs
- Mayonnaise

### Tonno & uova

Yields 3 square sandwiches.

- 1 can of tuna
- 2 medium eggs
- 1 small tomato
- Optionals: capers, chives, artichokes

### BLMT

Yields 2 square sandwiches.

- 3 slices of bacon
- Iceberg lettuce
- 1 small tomato
- Cream cheese
- 4 slices of thick mozzarella

### Cocktail di gamberetti

Yields 6-8 square sandwiches.

- 12 ounces of salad size cooked shrimp
- Chopped lettuce

## Directions

### Prosciutto & formaggio

1. Cook the mushrooms with 1 tablespoon of butter, salt, and herbs until browned.
2. Assemble in order: bread, mayonnaise, cream cheese, ham, mushrooms, sliced cheese, mayonnaise, bread.

### Tonno & uova

1. To boil the eggs, add to a small pot with cold water, measure 8 minutes from when it boils, then rapidly cool in a cold water bowl.
