Serving suggestion: sausages

## Ingredients

- 1 pound of spaghetti
- 2 pounds of zucchini, sliced thin (but not too thin)
- 65 grams of provolone, grated, or a mixture of pecorino and parmesan.
- 3 cloves of garlic
- Basil leaves
- Olive oil
- Salt and pepper

## Directions

1. Deep fry zucchini in batches in canola (2 cups) and olive oil (1 cup) until lightly browned.
2. Set aside on paper towels.
3. Sprinkle lightly with salt.
4. While the pasta is cooking, sauté the garlic lightly in 3 tablespoons olive oil, then remove.
5. When the pasta is done, re-add zucchini and pasta with 1 ¼ cups of pasta water and 1 teaspoon of salt. Reserve some zucchini for garnish.
6. Mix vigorously with the heat on for a minute or two.
7. Add the cheese and continue mixing off the heat until the cheese has melted into a creamy sauce, adding more pasta water as needed.
8. Serve and garnish.
