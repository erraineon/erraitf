## Ingredients

- 8 ounces spaghetti
- 1 garlic clove, cut in half
- 1 can (150-200 grams) tuna, drained
- 3 ounces cherry tomatoes, halved
- Handful of capers, rinsed and squeezed dry
- Handful of olives, pitted and chopped
- Red pepper flakes
- Fresh parsley, minced
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt

### Garnish

- Fresh basil leaves
- Drizzle of olive oil
- Black pepper

## Directions

1. Rub the garlic halves into the bowl, then mince and leave in.
2. Break up tuna in chunks and add to the bowl.
3. Mix in the rest of the ingredients without breaking the tuna too much.
4. Drain the pasta when it’s al dente and add directly to the bowl.
5. Sprinkle garnish.
