## Ingredients

- 1 cup of rinsed rice (yields about 2 ½ cups cooked rice)
- 1 cup of water
- ½ teaspoon of salt (or ½ teaspoon of dark soy sauce and ½ teaspoon of soy sauce)
- 2 teaspoons of olive oil or sesame oil
- ½ teaspoon of turmeric

## Directions

1. Add rice and liquids and stir well.
2. Cook in the instant pot with the Custom rice & grain setting: 5 minutes on high, 10 minutes natural release, then fast release.
3. Stir to fluff up and store covered.
