## Ingredients

- 1 cup of rice (yields about 2 ½ cups cooked rice)
- 1 cup of water
- ½ tsp of salt (or ½ tsp dark soy sauce and ½ tsp soy sauce)
- 2 tsp olive oil or sesame oil
- ½ tsp turmeric

## Directions

1. Cook in the instant pot with the Custom rice & grain setting: 5 minutes on high, 10 minutes natural release, then fast release.
2. Stir to fluff up and store covered on keep warm.
