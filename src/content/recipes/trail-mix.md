## Ingredients

- 100 grams of almonds
- 60 grams of pecans
- 60 grams of walnuts
- 40 grams of cashews
- 60 grams of dates or raisins
- 40 grams of dried mango
- 40 grams of dried pineapple
- TODO: add pepitas and sunflower seeds

## Directions

1. Cut large fruit.
2. Put everything in a jar.
