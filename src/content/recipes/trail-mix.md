Going for a 65% nuts and 35% fruit ratio:

## Ingredients

- 100 grams almonds
- 60 grams pecans
- 60 grams walnuts
- 40 grams cashews
- 60 grams dates or raisins
- 40 grams dried mango
- 40 grams dried pineapple
- TODO: add pepitas and sunflower seeds

## Directions

1. Cut large fruit.
2. Put everything in a jar.
