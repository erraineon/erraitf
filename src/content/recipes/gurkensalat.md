## Ingredients

- 2 English cucumbers, peeled and sliced thin
- ½ medium onion or 1 shallot, sliced thin
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ cup full fat sour cream
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 teaspoon white sugar
- 2 tablespoons white or red vinegar
- 1 teaspoon chopped dill
- 1 teaspoon chopped parsley
- ¼ teaspoon paprika, plus extra to coat
- Fresh ground pepper

## Directions

1. Make the dressing in a bowl.
2. Add cucumbers and mix.
3. Sprinkle some black pepper and extra paprika.
4. Chill in the fridge for about an hour.
