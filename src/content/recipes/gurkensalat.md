## Ingredients

- 2 English cucumbers, peeled and sliced thin
- ½ medium onion or 1 shallot, sliced thin
- 1 teaspoon of salt

### Dressing

- ½ cup of full fat sour cream
- 1 teaspoon of white sugar
- 2 tablespoons of white or red vinegar
- 1 teaspoon of chopped dill
- 1 teaspoon of chopped parsley
- Fresh ground pepper

### Garnish

- ¼ teaspoon of paprika as garnish

## Directions

1. Toss the cucumbers and onions with 1 teaspoon of salt in a colander and let sit for 30 minutes to draw out moisture.
2. Dry with a kitchen towel.
3. Make the dressing in a bowl.
4. Add cucumbers and mix.
5. Sprinkle some black pepper.
6. Chill in the fridge for about an hour.
