Hi, I'm Errai (_eh-RA-ee_, he/him, 34), an introverted developer, photographer, and digital artist forever drawn to the unknown. I hope we can become friends.

Originally from Rome, Italy, I moved to the US in 2019 and currently live in the dense and eclectic neighborhoods of Somerville, MA with my boyfriend, a brilliant freelance artist who goes by [Sumiaou](https://sumiaou.carrd.co).

Mystery and storytelling shape every aspect of my life. When I'm not writing code, I'm [reverse engineering it](/dev-journey). I devour music and cinema. While I enjoy drawing and composing music, most of my art is [photographic](/photos). I'm currently working on an unannounced indie video game project.

I am shy and curious in equal measure, but very talkative when I open up, especially if you match my flow. I like quiet places, and think a lot about time. I love being alive.

My avatar is some kind of gooey dragon. If you'd like to know more, check out [Errai's reference page](/reference-page). Add me on [Discord](https://discordapp.com/users/382774230267330567) or [Telegram](https://t.me/errai) if you'd like to chat!
